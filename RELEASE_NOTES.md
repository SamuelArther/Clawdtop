The big polish update. Dozens of little fixes all over Clawd, so he feels smooth and reliable.

**Getting him:** download `Clawdtop-v1.0.1.zip` below, unzip it anywhere, and double-click `Clawdtop.bat` (Windows)
or `Clawdtop.command` (Mac), or run `./Clawdtop.sh` (Linux). He needs Java 22 or newer (free from
[Adoptium](https://adoptium.net); on Windows the starter opens the page for you if it's missing or too old).
Already have him? He'll offer this update himself the next time he starts.

**Smoother**
- No more jumping back to his spot after you drag him, and game-corner mode stays put.
- No flickers when he walks to an app's spot, comes out of his box, or drops in for your birthday.
- Lands on the ground (not in it) when he's upside down; flips smoothly after a crash or a tackle.
- Tall hats fit, the guitar and bass fit, no extra hands while he holds his timer, the cake stays put.
- Speech bubbles wrap long lines and point at him even at the edge of the screen.
- Music plays on time and stops the moment he does (or when you mute him).

**Smarter**
- "Ask me a question": he knows today's date, keeps accents, never stops mid-sentence, and a question asked while his
  brain installs gets answered when it's ready (with download progress).
- Reminders understand "set a 5 minute timer", "in 1 hour 30 minutes" and more, and he's honest about the ones he
  can't do yet ("at 5pm").
- Weather and dice only when you mean them. Math he can't read out goes straight to Calculator.

**Kinder**
- Greetings come one at a time (and he wakes up for them). Focus mode stays on through reminders.
- "No tomfoolery" really turns off all the silly stuff. He stays quiet while you watch a video, and nothing's lost.
- He never codes or pops things up over your full-screen game.

**Safer**
- Settings never get lost when Clawd and the `clawd` command save at the same time.
- `clawd stop` only ever stops Clawd. Folder cleaning refuses network and USB drives (no Recycle Bin there).
- `clawd move` can't be guessed or faked by someone else on your wifi. Updates swap in whole.
- Works with names like "José" in your folders.
