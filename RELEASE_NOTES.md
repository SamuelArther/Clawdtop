Fix: two monitors (and side taskbars).

- Drag him to your other monitor and he stands on that monitor's taskbar. Before, he used the main monitor's height, so he could float in mid-air or drop out of sight.
- A spot on a monitor to the left of your main one is remembered (it used to jump back to a corner), and he starts on the right monitor.
- Unplug a monitor he's on, or change the resolution, and he comes back to a monitor you can see.
- He keeps out from under a taskbar or Mac Dock on the side of the screen.

Get him: download the zip, unzip, double-click Clawdtop.bat (Windows) or Clawdtop.command (Mac). Needs Java 22+.
