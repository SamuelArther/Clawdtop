Clawdtop 8.1: a big bug sweep (no new features).

- Music: his hands keep time with the real song (tempo changes and all), muting mid-song carries on instead of starting over, he stops when the music does, and sung parts sing in jams.
- Files you give him: safer when something goes wrong (huge zips or pictures, Mac zips, files that won't move).
- Tackling: a window you minimize while he's charging never gets stuck off-screen, and nothing's left hidden if he's stopped suddenly.
- Ask box: "notebook or laptop?" isn't saved as a note, "12 tonight" is midnight, timers go off during full-screen games.
- Plus fixes for OneDrive folders, second monitors, Mac and Linux.

Get him: download the zip, unzip, double-click Clawdtop.bat (Windows) or Clawdtop.command (Mac). Needs Java 22+.
