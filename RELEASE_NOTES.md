The first real release of Clawd, your little desktop crab.

**Getting him:** download `Clawdtop-v1.0.0.zip` below, unzip it anywhere, and double-click `Clawdtop.bat` (Windows)
or `Clawdtop.command` (Mac), or run `./Clawdtop.sh` (Linux). He needs Java 22 or newer (free from
[Adoptium](https://adoptium.net); on Windows the starter opens the page for you if it's missing).

**What he does:**
- Sits on your taskbar, follows your cursor with his eyes, naps, and talks in little beeps.
- Rides your cursor (right-click to drop him off, shake to fling him).
- Codes over 275 little programs of his own: a rainbow carpet, a rocket, a duck flood, and lots more.
- Tackles the taskbar icon of apps you open. They pop open and he goes flying.
- Plays piano, guitar, bass and drums. Drop a MIDI file on him and he plays the whole thing.
- Answers questions with a small AI that runs on your own computer (optional, set up for you).
- Timers, reminders, a focus timer, folder cleaning (to the Recycle Bin), and "How's my computer?".
- Spots for each app, hats and t-shirts, holidays, tic-tac-toe, a diary, and a "No tomfoolery" switch.

**Updates:** he checks for a newer version when he starts (once a day) and asks before updating. `clawd update`
does it from a terminal.
